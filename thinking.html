Chapter 02 — How I think

Notice. Question. Imagine.
Execute. Learn.

A working loop, not a process diagram. It starts with paying attention and ends with an honest look at what actually happened.

  1. 01

    Notice

    Pay attention to people, habits, conversations and the details others overlook.

  2. 02

    Question

    Find the consumer insight hiding behind an everyday behaviour.

  3. 03

    Imagine

    Turn that insight into a memorable idea that can travel across channels.

  4. 04

    Execute

    Bring people together, coordinate moving parts and get the idea into the world.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Look at what worked, understand why, and make the next iteration better.
