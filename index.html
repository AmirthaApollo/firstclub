01 — Why me

Three things I bring to
a brand team.

01

I look beyond the obvious.

My Economics and Finance background has trained me to think analytically, question assumptions, and understand the incentives and behaviours behind consumer decisions. I'm interested in why people choose certain brands, what makes them trust them, and what makes them come back.

02

I turn ideas into things.

From marketing campaigns and social media to websites and digital products, I enjoy taking an idea from its earliest stages to execution. I've worked across startups, collaborated with founders, and built projects independently.

03

I care about what works.

I enjoy combining creativity with data. I've analysed marketing campaigns, tracked engagement and conversions, built dashboards, and used performance insights to improve decisions. I don't think good marketing ends when something goes live.
