I'm Amirtha Apollo. I spend a lot of time noticing: how a queue forms, why a label makes someone reach for one jar over another, which ads people quote weeks later. Then I write about it, and usually build something to test the idea.

This is where I keep the work, the writing and the campaigns I can't stop thinking about. It's a brand marketing application for FirstClub. But mostly it's just how my head works.

A little about me

Curious by default, opinionated by dinner.

I studied Economics and Finance, which quietly turned me into someone who wants to know the "why" behind a purchase before the "what". That curiosity took me through consulting, a healthcare startup and a sustainable footwear brand, data on one side, brand and growth on the other.

Outside work, I build small things on the internet for the same reason other people do crosswords.
