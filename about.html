About me

I like problems that come with people attached.

Consulting taught me rigour. Startups taught me speed. Building things taught me that an idea isn't real until somebody actually uses it.

A little about me

The short, honest version.

I'm curious about people first and brands second, which is probably the right order. I want to know why someone switches, why they stay, and what a brand has to do to earn a place in their everyday. Most of my best ideas start as a small observation, not a big strategy.

I studied Economics and Finance, spent time in consulting and startups, and picked up a habit of building small digital things on the side. If I have an idea about how something should work, I can go and make a version of it.

I write, I overthink words, and I keep a running list of ads I think are genuinely good. This site is that list, plus the work that got me here.

"I'm less interested in what a brand says it is, and more in what people say it is."
